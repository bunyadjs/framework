# Code of conduct

We want Bunyad to be a welcoming project. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space agrees to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this code.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or open a private report through the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/bunyadjs/framework/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately.

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
